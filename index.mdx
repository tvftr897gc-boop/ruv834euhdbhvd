## Built for performance

Sculk game hosting runs on high-frequency AMD Ryzen 9 9950X hardware with DDR5 memory and NVMe storage. Plans include DDoS protection, flexible server software, panel access, and no artificial player-slot limit.

<Note>
  Your practical player capacity depends on your game, server software, plugins or mods, world size, and allocated CPU and memory.
</Note>
