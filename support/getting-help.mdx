# Getting help

Sculk Hosting provides 24/7 support from real people. Use the billing portal to open a support request tied to your account and service.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Open a support request" icon="ticket" href="https://my.sculkhosting.com">
    Contact the support team through the billing portal.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Join the community" icon="discord" href="https://discord.gg/sculkhosting">
    Connect with the Sculk Hosting community.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Include these details

- Your server identifier
- The game and server software version
- When the issue began
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Relevant console errors
- Changes made immediately before the problem

<Warning>
  Do not share your password, payment details, two-factor authentication codes, or recovery codes with anyone.
</Warning>
