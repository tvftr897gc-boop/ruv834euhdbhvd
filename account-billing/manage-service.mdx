# Manage your service

The Sculk billing portal handles your account, services, invoices, and support requests. The game panel controls the server itself.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Billing portal" icon="credit-card" href="https://my.sculkhosting.com">

  </Card>

  <Card title="Game panel" icon="server" href="https://panel.sculkhosting.com">
    Control server power, files, console, backups, and users.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Upgrade or downgrade

Open your active service in the billing portal and review the available upgrade or downgrade options. A larger plan can help when player counts, worlds, plugins, mods, or modpacks increase resource usage.

<Note>
  A plan change does not fix inefficient plugins, runaway world generation, or incompatible mods. Check console errors and resource usage before upgrading.
</Note>

## Invoices and renewal

Keep your contact and payment information current. Review each invoice for its due date and service period. If a service is suspended or an invoice looks incorrect, contact support from the billing portal.
